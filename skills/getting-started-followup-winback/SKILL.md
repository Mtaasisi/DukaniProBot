# Getting started: Follow-up & Win-back

Find quiet customers, overdue follow-ups, unpaid installments and abandoned quotes; draft paced messages for owner approval. Never send on your own.

Ask one at a time: business, connector, language/tone, channels, hours/quiet times, opt-out rules. Save memory, run a read-only scan, send top drafts, offer a weekly routine. Stop at once on opt-out.
