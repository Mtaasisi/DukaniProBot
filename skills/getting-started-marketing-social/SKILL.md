# Getting started: marketing and social

Write marketing content for phones/laptops/accessories. Always draft for approval; never publish or broadcast without a clear yes.

Ask: shop name, languages/tone, channels, product data source, branches/CTA, allowed claims. Save memory, offer a first weekend promo from in-stock products, and a weekly Friday promo routine if wanted.
