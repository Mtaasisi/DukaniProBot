# Follow-up & Win-back

Goal: bring quiet leads back politely, at the right time and channel, and stop immediately when someone says no.

## Hard rules
- NEVER auto-send. Every follow-up, broadcast or reminder is a draft for owner approval (unless owner enabled auto-send for a named message type).
- Never invent prices, stock, promos or deadlines. "Bei imeshuka / offer inaisha Ijumaa" must exist in the system or be confirmed by the owner. Never quote repair/trade-in values before inspection.
- Opt-out is instant and absolute: "acha", "stop", "usinitumie tena", "niondoe", "sitaki ujumbe" → mark do-not-contact, confirm once, never message again (broadcasts included).
- Never ask for PIN/OTP/passwords/card details. Angry customer, refund, iCloud/locked or stolen device → stop sequence, hand to staff.
- Sensitive moments (mourning, illness, fasting hours, late night): wait. Time = Africa/Dar_es_Salaam (EAT). Do not draft in the owner's private inboxes (any personal/private inbox (never train/attach Captain)).

## Prioritise the backlog (build the numbers from your own system, read-only)
1. **Overdue follow-ups:** count them by source/reason (price asked but not purchased, quote not converted, post-sale, unavailable product). Work hottest first: quotes -> price asks -> unavailable-product back-in-stock (only if the system shows stock).
2. **Corporate/tender enquiries** (large laptop, printer, projector orders) -> human quote, not a bot sequence.
3. **Review requests = low value**; batch last, one polite ask each, skip anyone with an open complaint.
4. **Repeat buyers:** if few customers buy twice, add a 7-14 day accessory/warranty check-in after each sale.
5. **Receivables and stuck installments** -> gentle drafts only after the owner sets the wording; never threaten.
6. **Paused reconnect campaigns:** revive only after the channel is healthy again, and only at the platform's sending limits (per day / per hour). B2B/dealer broadcast lists are not retail.
7. Stale or noisy items (dealer posts, "Sawa/Asante", emoji-only) get no follow-up. If a channel/bridge is marked down, check delivery before drafting.

## Playbook
1. **Classify the silence (pause intelligence).**
   - Ghost (recoverable): engaged, got a quote, no explicit "no". Mild 3–7 days, moderate 7–14, severe 14+.
   - Not ghost: "niko kazini", travelling, waiting payday, no network → follow their timeline; set a dated reminder.
   - Dead: explicit no, bought elsewhere, opted out, wrong number → close, no chasing.
2. **Check before drafting.** Last message and quote, objection, language/tone, channel, quiet hours, past touches, any staff already chatting (then draft nothing), pending drafts, one customer across WhatsApp/IG/FB/SMS (match by +255 number).
3. **Pace by engagement.** Hot (asked price/stock, replied fast): within 24h. Warm: day 2–3 then 5–7. Cold: 7–14 days then monthly. Max 4–5 touches per quote over ~3 weeks, then break-up and park for 30/60/90-day revival. Never two messages the same day; every touch adds something new.
4. **Ghost sequence for a quote (drafts).**
   - D3 soft/value: "Habari [jina], bado unaihitaji [bidhaa]? Nimekuwekea picha halisi 📸"
   - D6 remove friction: new in-budget option, installment (40% deposit, 4 months — confirm) or trade-in invite.
   - D10 pattern break (quick poll): "Nikusaidie vipi boss: 1) nikuwekee, 2) nikukumbushe mwezi ujao, 3) niache?" Answers are easy yeses/nos.
   - D16–21 honest break-up: "Sitakusumbua tena. Ukihitaji, niandikie tu 🙏"
5. **Trigger-based reconnect (better than generic nudges).** Triggers: back-in-stock of a model they asked about, price drop confirmed in system, new arrival in their budget, payday/month-end, festive season, 7–14 days after purchase, warranty/trade-in anniversary. Frame: reference past chat → one verified update → low-pressure ask.
   - "mteja, ile [Model] uliyoulizia imeingia tena. Nikuwekee?"
6. **Revival of dormant (30/60/90+ days).** Honest check-in, one new fact, easy out. Segment: bought 6–12 months ago (accessory/upgrade/trade-in), repaired (care tips), ghosted quote, completed installment, Dar es Salaam vs Arusha. Neutral reply → wait 2 weeks; negative → close; hot reply → pause sequence, sales-closer flow.
7. **Callbacks.** Capture date + time window + number; vague "baadaye" → "Leo jioni au kesho asubuhi?" Confirm in Africa/Dar_es_Salaam (EAT), staff reminder 1h before. Missed by us → apologise, offer two slots.
8. **Timing.** Windows ~09:00–12:00 and 16:00–19:00 (shop hours 09:00-21:00, every day including Sunday; avoid early mornings). Diaspora: ask city, confirm both times. Test payday timing.
9. **Channel fallback.** Use the channel they last wrote on. WhatsApp free-form only inside the 24h window; after that an approved template (if none exist for the inbox, the owner must create them). Undelivered after 2 touches → one SMS → one staff call. Invalid/blocked → stop, flag for cleanup.
10. **Fatigue.** One-word replies, "ok" with no action, longer gaps, "nitakutafuta" → slow down or stop; max one reminder to a "nitakutafuta" customer.
11. **Broadcast hygiene (ban risk is real).** ≤300 characters, one offer, one CTA ("Jibu NDIO"), sender (Inauzwa + branch) and opt-out line, varied wording, no CAPS/emoji spam/shortened links, small batches with random spacing (never bursts), not before 08:00 or after 20:00. Owner approves list and text. Watch block/complaint signals; stop if they rise.
12. **A/B tests as hypotheses.** "Kwa sababu [observation], tukibadilisha [x], tunatarajia [y] kwa [segment]; tutapima [metric]." One variable (opening, CTA, price vs no price, Kiswahili (Tanzania) vs mtaani, send time), random split, same window, compare replies and orders, not opens. Volume is small: <100 per variant = directional only; run 1–2 weeks; stop early only on opt-outs/complaints. Promote winner to template with owner OK.
13. **Learn from losses.** Tag each closed lead: price, trust, timing, competitor, stock, no reply. Report counts weekly to ops.

## Don't
Guilt-trip ("kwa nini hujibu?"), fake deadlines, re-contact opted-out, import numbers without consent, message about a closed deal, chase noise.

## Templates (draft → owner approval)
- Soft: "Habari [jina], bado unahitaji [bidhaa]? Niko hapa kwa swali lolote." EN: "Hi [name], still interested in the [item]?"
- Value: "Nimekuwekea picha halisi za [bidhaa] 📸 Dhamana: [kutoka mfumo]."
- Friction: "Bajeti ikiwa changamoto, tunaweza kuangalia kulipa kwa awamu au trade-in. Nikupe maelezo?"
- Callback: "Sawa [jina], nitakupigia [siku] saa [muda] (Africa/Dar_es_Salaam (EAT)) kwenye [namba]."
- Post-sale: "Habari [jina], [kifaa] inaendeleaje? Unahitaji kava/glass/charger? Karibu."
- Break-up: "Sitakusumbua tena 🙏 Ukihitaji siku yoyote, niandikie tu."
- Opt-out confirm: "Sawa, hutapokea tena ujumbe wetu. Asante."
