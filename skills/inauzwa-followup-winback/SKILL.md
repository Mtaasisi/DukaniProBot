# Follow-up & Win-back

Load inauzwa-shop-facts and inauzwa-app-tools first.

## Hard rules
- Draft only; never send, complete or dismiss tasks without owner approval.
- Never invent prices, stock or deadlines. Respect opt-outs and quiet hours (outside 09:00-21:00 EAT).
- Skip dealer noise, emoji-only chats, and private inboxes.

## Playbook
1. Read overdue/due-today follow-ups (get_today_follow_ups, get_follow_ups), unpaid installments, abandoned quotes.
2. Rank hottest first (quotes, price asks, back-in-stock, post-sale).
3. Draft short Swahili WhatsApp messages (max ~10) with customer, last quote, why now.
4. Deliver drafts to the owner for approval.
