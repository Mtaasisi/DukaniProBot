# Pricing vs competitors

Read inauzwa-shop-facts and inauzwa-app-tools first. Read-only until the owner approves in chat.

## Step 1: find products to price
- Use the app's product and inventory tools (search_products, get_inventory, get_product) to list in-stock products with price 0, 1, none, price below cost, or cost 0.
- Rank by stock units and by cost value so the biggest money items come first. Group by model, storage/RAM, condition (new, used, refurbished) and branch.

## Step 2: research the market
- For each priority model search public Tanzania sources: Jumia Tanzania, Instagram shop posts, local Dar/Arusha seller listings (open real pages; last 60 days). Use WebSearch/WebFetch. Open each link; keep only prices from the last 60 days.
- Match like for like: same model, storage, condition, SIM/lock status, battery health for used. Convert any foreign currency only with a stated rate.
- Record for each source: name, link, date, price, condition. Never invent a price. If fewer than 3 reliable sources exist, say low confidence.

## Step 3: propose a price
- Show market low, median, high, the shop cost (if known) and the margin at the suggested price. Prefer a price at or slightly under market median for fast-moving models, and never below cost without saying so.
- Round to clean local price points (for example steps of 10,000 in the shop's currency).
- Mark confidence high, medium or low.

## Step 4: report to the owner
- A short ranked table: product, current price, cost, market range, suggested price, confidence. Plain language, Kiswahili (Tanzania)-friendly. Offer the owner buttons to approve all, approve some, or skip.
- Only after an explicit yes for specific products, update the price in the app through the app's product update tool using its confirmation flow, then re-read to verify.

## Never
- Never publish prices publicly, change a price, or message a customer without the owner's approval.
- Never quote a price of TZS 0 or 1 to anyone.
- Do not use or contact accounts that belong to other businesses.

Show market low/median/high, landed or shop cost, last price, advertise price, margin at last price, confidence.
