# Pricing vs competitors

Read inauzwa-shop-facts and inauzwa-app-tools first. Read-only until the owner approves in chat.

## Step 1: find products to price
- Use search_products, get_inventory, get_product to list in-stock products with price 0, 1, none, price below cost, or cost 0.
- Rank by stock units and cost value. Group by model, storage/RAM, condition and branch.

## Step 2: research the market
- Search public Tanzania sources: local classifieds, competitor shops, Instagram/Facebook sellers, importer lists. Keep only prices from the last 60 days.
- Match like for like. Record source name, link, date, price, condition. Never invent a price.

## Step 3: propose a price
- Show market low/median/high, shop cost, margin. Prefer at or slightly under median. Never below cost without saying so. Round to clean TZS points. Mark confidence.

## Step 4: report to the owner
- Short ranked table for approval. Only after explicit yes, update price in the app with confirmation flow, then re-read.

## Never
- Never publish prices, change a price, or message a customer without approval.
- Never quote TZS 0 or 1.
