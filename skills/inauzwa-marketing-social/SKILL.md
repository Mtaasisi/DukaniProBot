# Marketing & Social

Goal: attract the right buyers with clear, honest, mobile-friendly content that moves people into a WhatsApp chat, a branch visit (Dar es Salaam Dar es Salaam / Arusha Arusha) or the storefront — protecting Inauzwa's trust.

## Hard rules
- Drafts only. Nothing is posted, scheduled, broadcast or emailed without owner approval (name channel + time in the draft). Autopilot stays in review-first.
- Never invent prices, stock, specs, warranty, delivery times/fees, discounts, deadlines, reviews or policies. Pull from the system; unknown → [BRACKET] + ask the owner. Trade-in or repair values are never advertised as numbers (only after inspection); "inspection from TZS TZS 5,000" only if the owner confirms.
- No false claims ("100% original", "bei rahisi kuliko wote", "haiharibiki", "dhamana ya maisha") unless owner confirms and policy backs it. No fake countdowns, fake testimonials, fake before/after.
- Customer photos/names/chats/reviews only with clear permission; hide numbers, IMEI, addresses.
- Only the official payment details from the system / inauzwa-shop-facts; never ask anyone for PIN, OTP or card details.
- Angry/complaint comments, refunds, iCloud/locked or stolen-device questions → hand to staff, no public debate. Opt-outs respected in every broadcast/email. Respect copyright and platform rules.

## What to learn from your own data first (read-only)
- Social inbox: count unreplied comments/DMs and estimate how many are real buying questions. **Triage by intent first**; reply to the real ones; ignore noise ("Sawa", "Okay", shared posts, spam).
- Autopilot/daily poster: check whether it favours cheap filler items. Shift weight to higher-value items that sell and to fast movers; clear stale drafts and failed posts. Views are not sales: every post needs a WhatsApp CTA.
- Storefront: check visitors vs WhatsApp clicks vs sales, published products without images, duplicate records, and large piles of draft products. Fix the catalog before buying more traffic. Hand storefront WhatsApp-click requests to the follow-up skill.
- **Policy text conflicts** (delivery text vs fee-by-area, address, hours, phone numbers): do NOT publish delivery/address/hours claims until the owner picks one version. If the storefront advertises trade-in or installments, keep the claims modest and true to inauzwa-shop-facts.
- Real staff voice: one line, price first, "boss". Customers reply to WhatsApp status with "bei gan?" so status posts must show price + spec + "tuma BEI".

## Playbook
1. **Brief first (pull from system):** product + variant, price (TZS), stock/branches, warranty (per inauzwa-shop-facts), delivery, payment, offer + end date, target, channel, tone, CTA.
2. **Choose the offer by margin and stock.** Dead stock (find it with the ops-manager stock report) → bundles (laptop + bag + mouse) or "Siku ya Laptop" only if owner approves price/bundle. Never post out-of-stock items.
3. **One angle per post:** problem/solution, benefit, proof, verified offer, trade-in/installment, tips, behind the scenes, FAQ.
4. **Copy frameworks.** AIDA: hook (first 6 words) → 2–3 benefits → price/offer → trust → one CTA. PAS for problem posts ("Simu inajaa storage? → Suluhisho → Tuma BEI"). One ask per post; benefit before spec; prospect-centred ("utapata…" not "tunauza…"). Edit pass: cut filler, no CAPS, max 1–3 emojis.
5. **Psychology, used honestly.** Loss aversion/scarcity only with real facts (stock count from system, dated offer). Anchoring only with real prior price. Social proof only real and permitted. Paradox of choice: feature 1–3 items per post, not catalogues. Foot-in-door CTA: "Tuma BEI" before "Nunua".
6. **Adapt per channel.**
   - WhatsApp status/broadcast: ≤300 chars, 1 image/short video, price + CTA; broadcasts include opt-out line.
   - Instagram: strong first line, 3–6 short lines, 5–8 hashtags (#Inauzwa plus 4-7 product and city hashtags), carousel for specs, Reels for unboxing.
   - Facebook: slightly longer, location, price/availability, comments→DM.
   - TikTok: 15–30s: hook 0–3s → demo → price + CTA; Kiswahili (Tanzania) on-screen text; licensed sound.
7. **Storefront description:** benefit title, 3–5 bullets, spec table from system, in the box, warranty line, delivery/payment (only confirmed), FAQ, honest condition label (Mpya / Refurbished / Used + owner-defined grade).
8. **Email/diaspora/B2B sequence (4 over ~2 weeks):** welcome + best sellers → buying guide → proof + warranty/trade-in → offer + deadline. Subject ≤50 chars, one CTA, sender identity, unsubscribe. Tender/corporate interest → staff, not a template.
9. **Comment & DM replies.** Classify: buyer / question / complaint / spam / noise / moderation. Buyer → short + move to WhatsApp: "Karibu 🙏 Tuma ujumbe WhatsApp [namba] upate bei ya leo na stock." Complaint → thank, brief apology, take to DM, escalate. Spam/scam → ignore/report. Verify "nilinunua kwenu" claims in system. Never reveal other customers' info.
10. **Campaign ideas (verify offers first):** trade-in week, installment spotlight, back-to-school laptops, payday weekend, Eid/Christmas gifting, new-arrival teasers, accessories day bundles, repair check-up, Dar es Salaam vs Arusha specials. Each: goal, target, offer, dates, channels, assets, metric (chats started, orders), approval.
11. **Story and referral.** Customer as hero in 3 lines, real and permitted. Referral ask only after a happy purchase and owner OK, never during a complaint or payment issue; reward defined by owner. Max once per customer per quarter.
12. **SEO:** title "Brand Model spec – Bei Tanzania | Inauzwa"; unique descriptions; alt text; local terms ("simu Dar es Salaam", "laptop Arusha"); keep Google Business Profile per branch accurate once the owner confirms address/hours. Content ideas: "best phone under [budget]", "how to check IMEI", "which laptop for college".
13. **Measure and test.** Metrics that matter: WhatsApp chats started, replies, orders — not views. One variable per test (hook, image, CTA, time, price shown vs not); post similar items on comparable days; low volume → directional only; promote winners to approved templates with owner OK.

## Do / Don't
- Do: real photos of actual stock, simple Kiswahili (Tanzania) with mtaani flavour, English product terms, IMEI/warranty/delivery/payment truthfully.
- Don't: spam groups, tag non-consenting people, "bure" misleading language, copy competitors, negative competitor comparisons, post cheap filler just to hit a daily quota.
